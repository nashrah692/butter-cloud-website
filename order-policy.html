Order Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

I'm a one-person, made-to-order home bakery. Everything here is a straightforward explanation of how ordering works, not a legal contract with accounts or subscriptions attached. If anything's unclear, just ask me on WhatsApp before you order.

Pre-order notice

Every order is baked from scratch after it's placed, so I need some lead time:

  • Standard orders: at least 1 day's notice.
  • Urgent orders: a minimum of 6 hours' notice, and only if there's room in that day's baking. Message me first to check before you count on it.

Changes & cancellations

You have a 1-hour window after placing your order to change or cancel it, plenty of time to double check the details. Once that hour passes, your order is locked in: I start planning around it and buying for it specifically, since I only ever bake one order's worth of ingredients at a time. That means cancellations aren't possible after the 1-hour window, and I'm not able to offer a refund for an order cancelled past that point.

If your delivery details turn out to be wrong

Delivery is currently only available within Multan, alongside pickup. If the address or phone number you give me is incomplete, incorrect, or unreachable and that causes a delay or a failed delivery, that's on the details provided rather than on me. I'm not responsible for the delay or non-delivery in that case. I'll always try to reach you to sort it out, but whether I can re-attempt delivery depends on my availability that day, and pickup may end up being the only option.

A few things I want to be upfront about

  • Everything is baked in a home kitchen that handles common allergens like eggs, gluten, dairy, and nuts. I can't currently accommodate eggless or other dietary restrictions, if that's a concern, please check with me before ordering.
  • Photos on this site are a guide, not a guarantee. Since every cake is handmade to order, small variations in look are normal, and elaborate custom designs aren't something I offer yet.
  • Cakes and bakes are perishable. Once your order is handed over, at pickup or delivery, how it's stored and how soon it's eaten is out of my hands.
  • If something genuinely goes wrong on my end, I'll make it right with a replacement or refund for that order. My responsibility doesn't extend beyond the value of the order itself.

Payment

Payment isn't collected through this website. It's arranged directly with me over WhatsApp, using bank transfer, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, SadaPay, or NayaPay.

Questions?

Message me on WhatsApp or Instagram any time. Before or after you order.
