Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

I'm a home baker, and this page explains what happens to your information when you use the order form on this site, plain and simple, no legal jargon.

What I collect

When you fill out the order form, I collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your delivery address; only if you choose delivery instead of pickup

That's it. I don't ask for payment details, accounts, or anything else through the form.

Why I collect it

I use this information for one reason: to make your order and get it to you. Your name and phone number let me confirm the details and reach you if anything needs clarifying, and your address lets me arrange delivery.

Who it's shared with

Your order details go to two places:

  • Formspree: the service that receives your order form submission and delivers it to me. Formspree has its own privacy policy covering how it handles that data.
  • WhatsApp: after you place an order, the form hands you off to WhatsApp to confirm it with me directly. WhatsApp is run by Meta and has its own privacy policy.

I don't sell your information, and I don't share it with anyone else or use it for marketing.

How long I keep it

I keep order details for as long as I reasonably need them, mainly to fulfil your current order and to keep a basic record in case of a mix-up or a repeat order. I don't hold onto it indefinitely, and I don't keep it any longer than necessary.

Your data, your call

Want me to delete the details I have for you, or check what I've kept? Just message me on WhatsApp and I'll sort it out, usually the same day.

Questions?

If anything here isn't clear, reach out on Instagram or WhatsApp and I'll happily explain.
