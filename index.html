the tea, spilled

hii 👋 here's the tea on why cakes aren't cheap. quick read, promise.

nothing's pre-made. your order gets baked when you order it, not sitting around for days pretending it's "fresh."

and "just buy in bulk" isn't a thing when you're one person making one order at a time. bulk ingredients would expire before i even touch half of them.

the oven and mixer don't run on vibes. i'm working around actual electricity timings. baking is not a "mix and done" situation.

still a student at COTHM, first year almost done 🎓, so yes, i stress over every cake. if it's not right, i redo it. your order gets my full attention, not leftovers.

that's the tea, fully spilled. now you know why i need a little advance notice: let's talk pre-orders 👇

so here's the math

A cake takes a few steps, and each one needs its time. Here's what happens between "order placed" and "cake ready":

  1. 1

    Mixing the batter

    25 min

    Sponge batter made from scratch, every single time.

  2. 2

    Baking the sponge

    35–40 min

    Low and slow, so it actually cooks through instead of just looking done.

  3. 3

    Cooling down

    ~60 min

    A warm sponge falls apart the moment you touch it. It needs time to set.

  4. 4

    Locking in the moisture

    a few hrs–overnight

    Cling wrapped and chilled so the sponge stays soft, not dry, by the time it's your cake.

  5. 5

    Soaking the sponge

    2+ hrs, or during coverage

    Three-milk sponges need at least 2 hours soaking before assembly. Simple sponges (pineapple, fudge, etc.) get soaked the moment coverage starts, with no separate wait.

  6. 6

    Prepping the fillings

    15–20 min

    Whipping cream, lotus spread, chocolate spread, crushed biscuit: whatever your cake calls for, made fresh.

  7. 7

    Covering & finishing

    up to 30 min

    The actual decorating: piping, spreading, making it look like your cake.

~5 hrs

of hands-on work, and that's before some cakes go back in the fridge to chill overnight. that's the pre-order math.

quick answers before you order

How far in advance do I need to order?

The sweet spot is at least 1 day's notice. The pre-order lowdown explains why.

Do you take same-day or urgent orders?

Sometimes! I need at least 6 hours' notice to bake, cool, and finish your cake. Message me first so I can check whether there's room for your order.

How do I actually place an order?

Through WhatsApp, the order form on this site, or Instagram DM, whichever's easiest for you.

What sizes do your cakes come in?

Bento, 1 pound, 2 pound, and 3.5 pound. The right size depends on your guest count and how generous you like your slices. Unsure? Message me and we'll work it out.

Can I customize the flavor or design?

I'm keeping customizations simple for now: think flavor swaps and small design tweaks. Elaborate designs aren't available yet, but send me your idea and I'll let you know what's possible.

Do you do eggless cakes or cater to dietary restrictions?

Not at the moment, but that might change down the line.

What payment methods do you accept?

Meezan Bank transfer, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, SadaPay, or NayaPay.

Do you deliver, or is it pickup only?

Both: delivery and pickup are available, but only within Multan for now.

Can I cancel or change my order after placing it?

You've got a 1-hour window after placing your order to make changes. After that, it's locked in, so it's worth double checking everything within that hour.

What goes into a butttercloud bake?

Your order is made fresh from scratch, with recipes I've tested and refined. From the sponge to the finishing touches, each bake gets my full attention. That's the care behind it.
